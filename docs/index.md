# Elsa Reichert - MEGR 2157 Portfolio

![MEGR 2156-7 Title](MEGR2156title.png)

This site is my working record of all my engineering assignments across MEGR 2157. These entry's follow the analyze, decide, communicate structure in accordance with standard engineering documentation practice. This allows the reader to see what I built and understood why I built it that way.

> **Engineering is the art of making decisions you can defend.**

## The three pillars

Most assignment pages are organized around:

- **Analyze** — the calculations, models, and data behind designs.
- **Decide** — the choices made and, critically, the reasoning for it.
- **Communicate** — the drawings, report, and presentation I'd hand to a client.

Use the navigation on the left to move between assignments.

- **Act II (Weeks 8–11):** Surveying the machine-element landscape and formally comparing options.
- **Act III (Weeks 12–15):** Building the complete lead screw translating system.
